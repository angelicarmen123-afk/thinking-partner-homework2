Thinking Partner Case Study

Topic: AI Coaching Tools vs. Human Coaching
Framework: The Seven Moves for Human-AI Dialogue
Move 1 — Ask a Real Question
"Set the record straight about AI-driven coaching tools replacing human coaches. There is a lot of talk about how AI can deliver the exact same transformation at 90% profit margins and low cost. What is a genuinely useful way to think about this?"
Personal Pushback 1 (On Financial Claims & Profit Margins):
"I need to push back on claims that AI tools allow coaches or platforms to operate at 90% profit margins effortlessly. As a coaching client, I know that real delivery requires continuous customer acquisition costs, software integration, platform fees, and ongoing human support. Claiming 90% profit margins ignores operational realities and marketing churn."

AI Response Summary: The AI acknowledged the pushback, agreeing that 90% margins ignore client acquisition and software maintenance costs. It clarified that we should separate routine task automation from deep human relationships.

Move 2 — Drill Deeper
"What does the latest research actually say about this? What does this look like over the next five to ten years, not just today?"

AI Response Summary: Research shows AI works well for simple daily habit tracking. However, for deep emotional growth, human coaches get much better results. In 5 to 10 years, AI will not replace human coaches completely, but will act as a daily assistant between sessions.

Move 3 & 4 — Explore Angles and Working Theories
"If you could ask any questions and explore every angle of this, what would you be most curious to ask? What working theories or hypotheses are we entertaining here?"

Working Hypotheses:

  • Hypothesis 1: AI works just as well as a human for organizing simple daily tasks.
  • Hypothesis 2: Clients will stop opening up to AI when facing serious emotional problems.
  • Hypothesis 3: Combining a human coach with an AI daily reminder tool works better than using only one or the other.
Move 5 — Force Calibration & Personal Pushback
"State your confidence on each claim as high, medium, or low, and name the assumptions behind each one."
Personal Pushback 2 (On Confidence Ratings & Client Experience):
"As someone who has been a coaching client, emotional breakthroughs rely on relational trust and real-time accountability that an LLM cannot recreate. Your confidence rating on AI replacing core human coaching functions feels unrealistically high. How do you justify that rating when psychological safety and real human life experience cannot be automated?"
Claim Confidence Main Assumption
AI matches humans in tracking simple daily tasks and goals. HIGH Assumes the client only needs structure and organization, not emotional support.
Standalone AI apps have high dropout rates after 90 days. HIGH Assumes people lose interest in software if there is no real person checking on them.
AI can replace human coaches for deep emotional personal growth. LOW Fails because software output cannot replace real human empathy and trust.
A hybrid approach (human coach + AI tools) gets the best results. MEDIUM Assumes software tools easily fit into a human coach's daily workflow.
Move 6 — Single Deep-Research Prompt
"Give me a single deep-research prompt that encompasses everything we covered."

Final Reusable Deep-Research Prompt:

"Perform a detailed study comparing AI coaching tools with human coaches. Look specifically at: (1) where AI is effective for basic daily habits, (2) why AI fails to build psychological safety and deep emotional trust, (3) calibrated confidence levels for each claim, and (4) how hybrid models will work over the next 5 to 10 years."
Move 7 — The Retrospective
"What prompt should I have used from the very start, and what would have been the most useful questions to ask?"

Ideal Starting Prompt:

"Act as an expert in psychology and personal development. Look critically at the claims that AI will replace human coaches. Clearly separate what AI can do for basic habit tracking from what it cannot do regarding human trust, psychological safety, and deep emotional changes. Provide confidence levels and explain your main assumptions."
